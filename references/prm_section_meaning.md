# PRM Section Meaning

Use this reference to explain `.prm` sections in geological terms. The table maps geological concepts to common ASPECT subsections and representative local case sources.

| Geological concept | ASPECT subsection | Common parameters or nested sections | Typical local case sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Model dimension | top level | `Dimension` | `cookbooks/convection-box/convection-box.prm`, `cookbooks/convection_box_3d/convection_box_3d.prm`, `cookbooks/shell_simple_3d/shell_simple_3d.prm` |
| Runtime and geological duration | top level | `Start time`, `End time`, `Use years in output instead of seconds`, `Maximum time step` | `cookbooks/convection-box/convection-box.prm`, `cookbooks/continental_extension/continental_extension.prm` |
| Model region | `Geometry model` | `Model name`; nested `Box`, `Spherical shell`, `Chunk`; `X extent`, `Y extent`, `Z extent`, `Inner radius`, `Outer radius`, `Opening angle` | `cookbooks/convection-box/convection-box.prm`, `cookbooks/shell_simple_2d/shell_simple_2d.prm`, `cookbooks/plume_2D_chunk/plume2D.prm` |
| Mesh resolution | `Mesh refinement`, `Discretization` | `Initial global refinement`, `Initial adaptive refinement`, `Strategy`, `Time steps between mesh refinement`, polynomial degrees | `cookbooks/convection_box_3d/doc/amr.part.prm`, `cookbooks/kinematically_driven_subduction_2d/doc/Case1_meshrefinement.prm`, `cookbooks/mid_ocean_ridge/doc/mesh_refinement.part.prm` |
| Gravity and buoyancy direction | `Gravity model` | `Model name`, `Magnitude`, nested `Vertical`, `Spherical constant`, `Radial constant`, `Function` | `cookbooks/convection-box/doc/gravity.part.prm`, `cookbooks/burnman/doc/gravity_model.part.prm`, `benchmarks/gravity_thin_shell/gravity_thin_shell.prm` |
| Plate or wall motion | `Boundary velocity model` | `Tangential velocity boundary indicators`, `Prescribed velocity boundary indicators`, `Zero velocity boundary indicators`, nested `Function`, `Ascii data model` | `cookbooks/continental_extension/doc/continental_extension_boundary_conditions.prm`, `cookbooks/kinematically_driven_subduction_2d/doc/Case1_velocity.prm`, `cookbooks/prescribed_velocity/corner_flow.prm` |
| Temperature boundary | `Boundary temperature model` | `Fixed temperature boundary indicators`, `List of model names`, nested `Box`, `Spherical constant`, `Function`, `Ascii data model`; `Top temperature`, `Bottom temperature` | `cookbooks/convection-box/convection-box.prm`, `cookbooks/kinematically_driven_subduction_2d/doc/Case2a_temperatures.prm`, `tests/boundary_temperature_function.prm` |
| Starting geotherm or thermal anomaly | `Initial temperature model` | `Model name`, nested `Function`, `Adiabatic`, `Perturbed box`, `Ascii data model`; `Function expression`, `Function constants` | `cookbooks/muparser_temperature_example/muparser-temperature-example.prm`, `cookbooks/plume_2D_chunk/plume2D.prm`, `cookbooks/initial-condition-S20RTS/S20RTS.prm` |
| Geological units and tracers | `Compositional fields` | `Number of fields`, `Names of fields`, `Types of fields`, `Compositional field methods` | `cookbooks/composition_active/composition_active.prm`, `cookbooks/composition_passive/composition_passive.prm`, `cookbooks/continental_extension/doc/continental_extension_composition.prm` |
| Initial rock domains | `Initial composition model` | `Model name`, nested `Function`, `Ascii data model`, `World builder`; `Variable names`, `Function expression` | `cookbooks/continental_extension/doc/continental_extension_composition.prm`, `cookbooks/subduction_initiation/subduction_initiation_compositional_fields.prm`, `tests/ascii_data_initial_composition_2d_box.prm` |
| Material rheology and density | `Material model` | `Model name`; nested `Simple model`, `Visco Plastic`, `Multicomponent`, `Melt global`; `Viscosity`, `Minimum viscosity`, `Maximum viscosity`, `Densities`, `Angles of internal friction`, `Cohesions` | `cookbooks/continental_extension/doc/continental_extension_material_model.prm`, `benchmarks/viscoelastic_plastic_shear_bands/kaus_2010/kaus_2010_extension.prm`, `cookbooks/steinberger/steinberger.prm` |
| Weak zone or shear zone | `Compositional fields`, `Initial composition model`, `Material model` | field names, function expressions, friction/cohesion/viscosity contrasts | `benchmarks/shear_bands/shear_bands.prm`, `benchmarks/finite_strain/simple_shear.prm`, `cookbooks/transform_fault_behn_2007/transform_fault_behn_2007.prm` |
| Internal, shear, adiabatic, latent, or radiogenic heating | `Heating model` | `List of model names`, nested heating model parameters | `cookbooks/latent-heat/latent-heat.prm`, `cookbooks/future/radiogenic_heating.prm`, `tests/adiabatic_heating.prm` |
| Free surface or surface deformation | `Mesh deformation`, `Free surface`, `Initial topography model` | `Mesh deformation boundary indicators`, topography model names, stabilization settings | `cookbooks/free_surface/free_surface.prm`, `cookbooks/free_surface_with_crust/free_surface_with_crust.prm`, `benchmarks/free_surface_tractions/viscous/free_surface_viscous_cylinder_2D_loading.prm` |
| Particles and Lagrangian markers | `Particles` | `Number of particles`, `Particle generator name`, `List of particle properties`, nested `Generator` | `cookbooks/convection-box-particles/convection-box-particles.prm`, `cookbooks/grain_size_ridge/doc/particles.part.prm`, `benchmarks/finite_strain/simple_shear.prm` |
| Melt or two-phase flow | `Melt settings`, `Material model`, `Postprocess` | `Include melt transport`, porosity fields, melt material properties, melt postprocessors | `cookbooks/global_melt/global_melt.prm`, `cookbooks/mid_ocean_ridge/mid_ocean_ridge.prm`, `benchmarks/solitary_wave/solitary_wave.prm` |
| Output figures and diagnostics | `Postprocess`, `Visualization` | `List of postprocessors`, `List of output variables`, `Output format`, `Time between graphical output` | `cookbooks/convection_box_3d/doc/postprocess.part.prm`, `cookbooks/kinematically_driven_subduction_2d/doc/Case1_postprocessing.prm`, `cookbooks/composition_active/doc/postprocess.part.prm` |
| External plugins | top level and plugin subsections | `Additional shared libraries`, plugin `Model name` values | `cookbooks/vankeken_subduction/vankeken_corner_flow.prm`, `benchmarks/finite_strain/simple_shear.prm`, `benchmarks/davies_et_al/case-2.3.prm` |

When explaining a file, keep the order of the `.prm` but translate each section into what a geologist can inspect or modify. If a subsection is not in this table, mark its geological meaning as `unknown` rather than inventing one.
